# Cheat sheet

Cheats are for single-player. Hosted and joined co-op games refuse them.

## How to use them

1. Press the **` / ~** key (below Escape) to open the console.
2. Type a command and press **Enter**.
3. Press **Escape** or **`** again to close it. Up and Down recall earlier commands.

Type `help` in the console for the full list. Nothing changes until you enter a command, and what a cheat gives you is kept in your save.

## Health and protection

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `heal` | Restores the health and Force of the character you control. Does not revive a dead character. |
| `invulnerability` | Turns damage protection on or off. |
| `turbo` | Turns faster party movement on or off. |

## Items, credits and supplies

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `giveitem ITEM` | Adds one of that item to your inventory. Add a number for more: `giveitem g_i_medeqpmnt01 5`. |
| `givecredits AMOUNT` | Adds credits. |
| `givemed` | Adds medpacs. |
| `giverepair` | Adds repair kits. |
| `givecomspikes` | Adds computer spikes. |
| `givesecspikes` | Adds security spikes. |
| `givesitharmour` | Adds the Sith armour outfit. |
| `infiniteuses` | Turns free use of usable items on or off. |

`giveitem` uses the game's own item names. For example, `giveitem g_w_lghtsbr01` adds a lightsaber and `giveitem g_a_jedirobe01` adds a Jedi robe. The item goes into your inventory; equip it from the equipment screen as usual.

## Experience and levels

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `addexp AMOUNT` | Adds experience to the party. |
| `addlevel` | Gives enough experience for the next level. You still choose the level-up yourself. |

## Light side and dark side

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `addlightside AMOUNT` | Moves your alignment toward the light side. |
| `adddarkside AMOUNT` | Moves your alignment toward the dark side. |

## Getting around

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `revealmap` | Reveals the map of the area you are in. |
| `whereami` | Shows the area's name and your position. |
| `warp AREA` | Travels to another area by its name, for example `warp tar_m02aa`. |
| `bright` | Turns full brightness on or off. |
| `restartminigame` | Restarts the minigame you are in. |

## Extras

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `dancedancemalak` | Switches on the original game's final-battle Easter egg. |

## Robes and outfits

These need extra mod files that are **not** in the download. Without them the game answers that the item is unknown.

| Outfit | Command |
|---|---|
| Darth Revan's robes | `giveitem ka_revan_dark` |
| Jedi Revan's robes | `giveitem ka_revan_light` |
| KOTOR II Jedi Knight robes | `giveitem ka_jedi_knight` |
| KOTOR II Jedi Master robes | `giveitem ka_jedi_master` |

Both Revan outfits can be worn at any alignment.

## Notes

- These commands were checked in KOTOR I. They have not all been checked in KOTOR II.
- Cheats can break quests if you skip ahead with `warp`. Save first.

# AI disclosure

KOTOR Academy was made with AI tools. Where AI was used:

**Tools:** Claude Code (Anthropic Claude models) and OpenAI Codex.

**Code:** KOTOR Academy's changes to the kjk engine, its launcher and its test scripts were written with AI coding tools under my direction. This covers the combat presentation and balance, the fixes to both campaigns, Manaan's underwater play, the practice arena and save handling. The kjk engine it is built on is the kjk contributors' work.

**Text:** this page, the README and the combat guide were drafted with the same tools. The rulebook is generated by the engine from its own rule files. I am responsible for their accuracy.

**Testing:** automated playthroughs of both campaigns were run and read by AI tools to find and fix problems. They do not replace play by hand, and not every scene has been played by hand.

**Game content:** the download contains no AI-generated voices, dialogue, music, models or textures. Everything you see and hear in the game comes from your own copies of the games and from Movie Duels.

**Launcher art:** the cover picture shown in the launcher's game picker window was made with an AI image tool. It is the only AI-generated picture in the download and it is not used inside the game.

**What I did:** I set the goals, played and tested in game, chose how combat looks and feels, approved the balance, and decided what ships.
